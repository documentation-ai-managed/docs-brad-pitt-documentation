## Overview

Operate your FXZIG trading robots effectively to maximize profitability. These expert advisors (EAs) run on MetaTrader 4 or 5 platforms, delivering up to 20 points expectancy per trade. Follow this guide to monitor performance, scale across accounts, adapt to markets, and apply best practices for sustained results.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Always run EAs on a VPS for 24/7 uptime and minimal latency.
</Callout>

## Monitoring Live Trades and Performance

Track your robots' activity in real-time to ensure optimal execution.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to Broker" icon="link">
    Launch MT4/MT5 and log in to your live account. Verify server connection shows green status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach EA" icon="download">
    Drag the FXZIG EA from Navigator to a chart (e.g., EURUSD H1). Enable "Allow live trading" in settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Journal" icon="book-open">
    Check the Experts and Journal tabs for trade logs. Look for errors like `>0 spread` or connection issues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze Stats" icon="bar-chart">
    Use the Trade tab to view profit factor, drawdown, and win rate. Aim for `>1.5` profit factor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Metric          | Target Value     | Description                          |
|-----------------|------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Profit Factor   | `{>1.5}`        | Gross profit divided by gross loss   |
| Max Drawdown    | `{<10%}`        | Largest peak-to-trough decline       |
| Win Rate        | `{>60%}`        | Percentage of profitable trades      |
| Expectancy      | `{20 points}`   | Average profit per trade             |

## Handling Market Conditions and Adjustments

Adapt to volatility spikes or news events without halting trades.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="High Volatility (e.g., NFP)" default-open="true">
    Increase `MaxSpread` to `5.0` points. Pause new trades during `{30min}` pre/post news.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Low Liquidity Periods">
    Set `MinEquity` threshold to avoid overtrading. Review Asian session performance weekly.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Never manually close EA trades—let risk management handle stops.
</Callout>
